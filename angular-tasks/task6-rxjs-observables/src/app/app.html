RxJS Reactive Programming vs Promises / async-await

💡 Promise vs RxJS Observable Mental Shift:
• Promise: Emits a SINGLE value in the future, non-cancellable.
• RxJS Observable: Emits a CONTINUOUS STREAM of multiple values over time, cancellable.
• Async Pipe (items$ | async): Angular automatically subscribes and unsubscribes!
